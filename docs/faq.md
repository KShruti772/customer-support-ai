# Frequently Asked Questions - AstraHome Support

## General Questions

### Q: How do I reset my password?
**A**: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your email or username, and you'll receive a password reset link via email. If you don't receive the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support@astrahome.com.

### Q: How do I contact AstraHome support?
**A**: 
- Email: support@astrahome.com (response within 2 business days)
- Phone: 1-800-ASTRA-HOME (8AM-8PM EST, Monday-Friday)
- Live chat: support.astrahome.com (available 24/7 for basic inquiries)
- Mail: AstraHome Support, 123 Tech Park Drive, San Jose, CA 95134

### Q: How do I create an account?
**A**: Download the AstraHome app and select "Sign Up". You'll need to provide:
- Email address
- Create a password
- Accept terms of service and privacy policy
- Verify your email via the link sent to your inbox

### Q: Does AstraHome sell customer data?
**A**: No. AstraHome does not sell customer data to third parties. Personal information is used solely for account management, service provision, and as described in our privacy policy. We may share data with trusted service providers who perform functions on our behalf, under strict confidentiality agreements.

## Account & Login

### Q: I'm locked out of my account after too many failed login attempts
**A**: Account lockout lasts 30 minutes for security reasons. After 30 minutes, you can attempt to log in again. If you continue to have issues, contact support@astrahome.com.

### Q: Can I have multiple users on one account?
**A**: Yes. The primary account holder can add family members or shared users through the AstraHome app. Each shared user has their own login and can view only the cameras and settings granted access to.

### Q: How do I change my email address?
**A**: Go to Account → Profile → Edit Email in the AstraHome app. You'll receive a verification link at the new email address. The change is finalized after clicking the link.

### Q: I forgot my registered email address
**A**: Contact support@astrahome.com with your order number and name. They can look up your account associated with your purchase.

## Product Questions

### Q: Which WiFi frequency should I use for my camera?
**A**: Cameras support 2.4GHz only. While 5GHz offers faster speeds, it has shorter range and is not compatible with most security cameras. Use 2.4GHz for reliable connectivity.

### Q: How many cameras can I have on one account?
**A**: 
- Free tier: Up to 3 cameras
- Basic subscription: Up to 5 cameras
- Pro subscription: Up to 10 cameras
- Premium subscription: Unlimited cameras

### Q: Do I need a subscription to use the cameras?
**A**: No, basic camera functionality works without a subscription:
- Live view
- Motion detection alerts
- Local storage to MicroSD card
- However, cloud video storage and advanced features require a subscription

### Q: How long is video stored in the cloud?
**A**: 
- Basic subscription: 1 month
- Pro subscription: 3 months
- Premium subscription: 1 year

### Q: Can I view my cameras on multiple devices?
**A**: Yes. The same account can be accessed from multiple smartphones, tablets, and computers. Each device must log in with the account credentials.

## Privacy & Security

### Q: Who can view my camera feeds?
**A**: Only people you explicitly grant access to through the AstraHome app. The primary account holder can add shared users with specific permissions. AstraHome employees cannot view your cameras without your permission or a legal request.

### Q: Does the camera record audio?
**A**: Yes, AstraCam models include built-in microphones. Audio recording can be enabled or disabled in the camera settings. A blue LED indicator shows when the microphone is active.

### Q: Does the camera have a physical privacy cover?
**A**: Yes, AstraCam X2 includes a physical camera shutter that slides over the lens. AstraCam X1 does not have a physical shutter but has a software mute switch.

## Subscription Questions

### Q: What happens if my subscription payment fails?
**A**: You'll receive an email notification. Access to cloud features is suspended until payment is updated. You have 7 days to update payment before the account is downgraded to the free tier.

### Q: Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?
**A**: Yes. Prorated refund is issued for the remaining monthly time, and the annual subscription begins immediately. Contact billing@astrahome.com to switch.

### Q: Do subscriptions auto-renew?
**A**: Yes, all subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the billing date. You can cancel anytime through the AstraHome app or by contacting support.

### Q: What's the difference between annual and monthly billing?
**A**: Annual billing typically offers 2 months free compared to monthly pricing. Annual subscriptions require a 1-year commitment, while monthly subscriptions can be canceled anytime.

## Troubleshooting

### Q: My camera is showing as "offline" in the app
**A**: 
1. Check if the camera has power (LED indicator)
2. Verify WiFi connection - camera requires 2.4GHz
3. Restart the camera (unplug 10 seconds, plug back in)
4. Check your internet connection
5. If still offline, reset the camera to factory settings

### Q: I'm getting too many motion alerts
**A**: 
1. Adjust motion detection sensitivity in camera settings
2. Set motion zones to ignore areas with frequent movement (streets, trees)
3. Schedule motion detection for specific hours
4. Use "Motion Off" periods (e.g., nights, work hours)

### Q: Can I share my camera access with family?
**A**: Yes. In the AstraHome app, go to Settings → Shared Users → Add User. Enter the family member's email address. They'll receive an invitation email to create their own account with access to your cameras.

### Q: Can I use AstraHome cameras with other smart home systems?
**A**: 
- Amazon Alexa: Full integration (view camera feeds on Echo Show, voice commands)
- Google Assistant: View on Google Nest Hub, voice commands
- Apple HomeKit: Not currently supported
- SmartThings: Limited integration via Alexa
