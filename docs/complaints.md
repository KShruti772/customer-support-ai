# Complaints & Escalation - AstraHome Support

## How to File a Complaint

### Submit a Complaint

**Email**: complaints@astrahome.com

**Information to include**:
- Order number (required)
- Product name and model (required)
- Detailed description of the issue (required)
- Photos or videos of the problem (if applicable)
- Your contact information (optional but helpful)
- Desired resolution

**Online form**: Visit support.astrahome.com/complaint and complete the submission form

**Phone**: 1-800-ASTRA-HOME (available 8AM-8PM EST, Monday-Friday)

### Complaint Acknowledgement

- You will receive an email acknowledgment within 2 business days
- A complaint ID will be assigned and included in the acknowledgment
- Initial response from customer service within 5 business days
- Full resolution timeline: typically 10-15 business days, depending on complexity

### What Happens After Submission

1. **Review**: Complaints team reviews within 2 business days
2. **Classification**: Issue categorized (product quality, shipping, billing, performance, etc.)
3. **Acknowledgment**: Email sent with complaint ID and expected timeline
4. **Investigation**: Relevant department investigates (quality, logistics, billing, etc.)
5. **Resolution**: Proposed resolution communicated via email
6. **Confirmation**: Customer confirms or provides feedback on resolution

### Complaint Categories & Typical Resolution Times

| Category | Typical Resolution | Escalation Trigger |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Product quality | 5-7 business days | Issue affects safety or multiple units |
| Shipping damage | 3-5 business days | High-value item or repeat occurrence |
| Billing errors | 3-5 business days | Involves disputed charges over $100 |
| Performance issues | 7-10 business days | Requires technical investigation |
| Installation problems | 5-7 business days | Requires professional assessment |

### Escalation Process

If your complaint is not resolved within the stated timeline, or if you are dissatisfied with the resolution:

**Step 1**: Request escalation via reply to the complaint acknowledgment email
- Your complaint will be reassigned to a senior specialist
- Response within 3 business days

**Step 2**: Management review
- If still unresolved, request escalation to management
- Response within 5 business days
- Management may offer: full refund, account credit, replacement product, or service extension

**Step 3**: Executive office
- Final escalation level
- Response within 7-10 business days
- May include: significant account credit, product replacement, or public apology

### Complaint Handling Principles

- **Fairness**: All complaints treated equally regardless of customer status
- **Timeliness**: Commitment to response timelines above
- **Accountability**: Clear complaint ID tracking throughout process
- **Confidentiality**: Customer information handled according to privacy policy
- **Continuous improvement**: Complaints used to improve products and processes

### What Is NOT Considered a Complaint

The following are handled through different channels:

| Issue | Correct Channel |
|-------|----------------|
| Product questions | support@astrahome.com |
| Technical setup | support@astrahome.com |
| Subscription questions | billing@astrahome.com |
| Returns (non-defective) | support@astrahome.com |
| General inquiries | contact@astrahome.com |

### Feedback Improvement

All complaints are reviewed weekly by the product team to identify:
- Recurring issues
- Product design improvements
- Process improvements
- Training needs for customer service

Anonymous trend data is shared with the product team weekly.
