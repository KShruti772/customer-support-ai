# Refunds & Returns - AstraHome Support

## Refund Eligibility

### Hardware Products

**30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**
- Applies to all AstraCam and AstraPlug products purchased directly from AstraHome
- Product must be in original condition with all accessories
- Original packaging is preferred but not required
- Damage from misuse or accidents is not covered

**How to Request a Refund**
1. Contact support@astrahome.com with your order number
2. Include photos of the product condition
3. State the reason for return
4. A prepaid return shipping label will be emailed within 2 business days
3. Ship the product within 14 days of receiving the label
4. Refund is processed within 5-7 business days after receipt

### Exceptions Not Covered

- Products used beyond 30 days from delivery
- Missing accessories or original packaging
- Damage from improper installation or accidents
- Products modified or tampered with
- Software or subscription fees (see below)

### Installation Services
- Professional installation fees are non-refundable
- Workmanship warranty: 90 days from installation date
- Issues due to incompatible wiring or network conditions are not covered

## Subscription Refunds

### Pro-Rated Refunds

- Cancellations during billing period: unused portion is pro-rated
- Example: Cancel halfway through a monthly plan = 50% refund
- Annual plan cancellations: refund for full remaining months
- Promotional discounts are pro-rated based on the discounted amount

### Non-Refundable Items

- Extended warranty purchases (after 14-day cooling-off period)
- One-time setup or installation fees
- Penalty fees for early termination
- Refunds for policy violations or abuse of the return program

## Return Process

### Step 1: Initiate
- Email support@astrahome.com with "Refund Request" in subject
- Include: order number, reason for return, photos if applicable
- Customer service will confirm eligibility within 2 business days

### Step 2: Ship
- Use the prepaid shipping label provided
- Include all accessories and documentation
- Ship within 14 days of label issuance
- Keep the tracking number for your records

### Step 3: Receive Refund
- Inspection: 3-5 business days after product receipt
- Refund method: original payment method (3-5 business days processing)
- Email confirmation sent when refund is processed
- See full bank statement update: 5-10 business days

## Complaints & Escalation

### How to File a Complaint

1. Email complaints@astrahome.com with a detailed description
2. Include: order number, product, issue description, photos/videos if applicable
3. Initial response within 2 business days
4. Full investigation within 5 business days

### Escalation Rules

**Level 1**: Customer service representative (initial contact)
- Response time: 2 business days
- Resolution scope: standard issues, returns, basic troubleshooting

**Level 2**: Senior support specialist
- Escalation trigger: Level 1 cannot resolve, or issue involves $200+ value
- Response time: 3 business days
- Resolution scope: complex technical issues, partial refunds

**Level 3**: Management/Executive escalation
- Escalation trigger: Level 2 cannot resolve, or issue involves $500+ value, or customer requests executive contact
- Response time: 5 business days
- Resolution scope: full refunds, account credits, significant policy exceptions

### Escalation Contact

If you have not received a response within the stated timeframes, or if your issue remains unresolved, you may:
- Call 1-800-ASTRA-HOME and ask for the escalations department
- Email executive-relations@astrahome.com
- Write to: AstraHome Customer Escalations, 123 Tech Park Drive, San Jose, CA 95134
