# Billing & Subscriptions - AstraHome Support

## Payment Issues

### Common Billing Questions

**"I was charged twice for my subscription"**
- This can occur if the subscription was renewed on both the old and new billing cycles
- Check your bank statement for duplicate charges 3-5 days apart
- Contact support@astrahome.com with the transaction IDs for a refund

**"My payment was declined"**
- Ensure your payment method has sufficient funds
- Verify the card expiration date is current
- Update your payment method in the AstraHome app
- If using a new card, the first transaction may be flagged as suspicious - this is normal

**"I canceled but was still charged"**
- Subscriptions renew up to 24 hours before the billing date
- Cancellation immediately stops auto-renewal, but access continues until the end of the billing period
- Refunds for canceled subscriptions must be requested within 30 days of the charge

### Invoice Requests

**"How do I get an invoice"**
- Invoices are automatically emailed at the start of each billing cycle
- To request a past invoice, contact support@astrahome.com with your order number
- Invoices are also available for download in the AstraHome app under "Billing History"

### Subscription Management

**"How do I upgrade my subscription"**
- Go to Account → Subscriptions in the AstraHome app
- Select the new plan and confirm
- Upgrades take effect immediately; remaining time on current plan is credited

**"How do I downgrade my subscription"**
- Same process as upgrading; select the lower-tier plan
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period
- You'll retain access to current plan features until the downgrade date

**"How do I cancel my subscription"**
- Go to Account → Subscriptions → Cancel Subscription
- Cancellation stops auto-renewal immediately
- You retain access until the end of your paid billing period
- Refunds must be requested within 30 days of cancellation

### Taxes

- Sales tax is calculated based on your billing address
- VAT/GST applies for customers in applicable regions
- Tax-exempt customers should submit documentation to billing@astrahome.com
